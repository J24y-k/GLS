Questions

Maintenance questions

What does a maintenance service cover?

Inspection of filters, refrigerant levels and electrical wiring, cleaning of critical components, and performance optimisation.

Does maintenance prevent breakdowns?

Regular servicing finds worn or dirty components before they fail, which reduces breakdowns and extends the life of the system. It cannot rule out every fault.

How often should a system be serviced?

That depends on the system, how hard it runs and where it is installed. GLS can recommend a schedule once the equipment has been assessed.

Does GLS maintain both refrigeration and air conditioning?

Yes, for homes and businesses.
