Questions

Air-conditioning questions

Which air-conditioning systems does GLS install?

GLS installs split-unit and ducted air-conditioning systems for homes and commercial properties, including offices and server rooms.

Does GLS repair air conditioners outside normal hours?

Yes. Emergency repair is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 073 654 1480 or message 061 934 2182 on WhatsApp.

Can GLS replace an old system with a more efficient one?

Yes. System upgrades cover energy-efficient and inverter units, eco-friendly refrigerants and smart climate control. A free site assessment is the starting point.

Which areas does GLS serve?

Johannesburg and surrounding areas, from our base in Primrose, Germiston.
